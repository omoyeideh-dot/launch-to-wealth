Meet Vanessa

Built by a woman who has lived every lesson she teaches.

Vanessa Adekoya worked at one of the world's largest consulting firms before she realized that the work she did every day, though high paying, was only making large corporations larger — and almost never making a dent on the people she truly cares about impacting.

So, she built a bridge out. Today, Vanessa teaches professionals around the world how to turn their expertise into income streams that don't depend on a single paycheck.
